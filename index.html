Why We Exist

We started Garnet Cards with a simple belief: celebrating people shouldn't cost a fortune. Greeting cards have gotten unreasonably expensive, so we're bringing things back to basics with affordable, high-quality handmade and printed options.

Giving Back: We believe in supporting the community that supports us. That's why we allocate a portion of our profits to local causes that create meaningful educational opportunities.
